ABOUT

The Sierra Stallone project is a (placeholder) name for what my works are packaged under. I want it to stand for people who have been brainwashed to (unfortunately) believe that you can essentially only do one thing with your life––one career, one art form, etc. It also, obviously, exists to be enjoyed by anyone and everyone!!

HOW YOU CAN HELP

WORK HARD AND PRODUCE THINGS. NO MATTER WHAT THEY ARE. ACADEMIA, ART, MUSIC, WRITING, JUST MAKE IT. THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS A WASTE OF TIME WHEN IT COMES TO THESE––THE INTERNET ALLOWS US TO FINALLY COMMUNICATE CONCEPTS AND CREATIVE IDEAS IN A FASHION NEVER SEEN BEFORE. IT IS OUR DUTY TO CONTINUE TO PRODUCE, IF EVEN AN OUNCE OF WHAT YOU CREATE CAUSES INSPIRATION, MOTIVATION, OR SPARKS ANY FORM OF INTEREST OR THOUGHT, IT IS FAR MORE IMPORTANT TO HUMANITY THAN YOU THINK.

One of the best things an individual can do for their mental health is to create. It is cathartic and, generally, beautiful. Do it!
